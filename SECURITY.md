# Security Policy

This project processes user-provided Word documents and generated images. Treat all templates, rosters, generated reports, and screenshots as potentially private.

## Reporting Issues

Please open a private security advisory or contact the maintainers before publicly disclosing issues that could leak private report content, identity fields, local paths, credentials, or generated artifacts.

## Handling Private Data

- Do not commit `input/`, `output/`, or `work/`.
- Do not commit rosters with names, student IDs, classes, or other personal data.
- Do not publish generated reports unless they have been reviewed and authorized for release.
- Do not publish templates or reference screenshots without permission.
